This is a fictional airline document used to demonstrate airline-specific retrieval filters. Reroute Air does not exist.

## Rebooking after disruption

If your Reroute Air flight is cancelled or delayed by more than three hours, you may rebook free of charge on any Reroute Air flight to the same destination within 14 days, or on a partner train for journeys under 600 km.

## Meal vouchers

Passengers receive a €15 meal voucher for delays over two hours and a €30 voucher for delays over four hours, available at the transfer desk or in the app.

## Claims process

Compensation claims must be submitted through the Reroute Air claims form with your booking reference. Reroute Air aims to respond within 30 days and pays approved claims by bank transfer within 14 days of approval.
